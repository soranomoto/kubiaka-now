aromia_bungii_japan_municipalities.csv  Municipalities by category in the national control strategy, reference 2 (11 Sep 2026).
  category: Established / Front line / Outlier / Adjacent, not infested. lat/lon = an approximate point for the municipality (geocoded), not the place of damage.
aromia_bungii_japan_new_detections.csv  First detections reported after that list. 'no' is assigned oldest first and never changes. what_ja is a Japanese summary of the source.
aromia_bungii_japan_rewards.csv  Municipal rewards for killed adult beetles, each read from the original page. yen_per_beetle is blank for prizes and lotteries.

lg_code: 6-digit local government code (with check digit), from code4fukui/localgovjp (CC0).
Romanised municipality names are converted automatically from kana readings (Hepburn, without macrons) and may differ from the municipality's own English spelling.
